# Project Proposal: PikiStreams

**Date:** January 15, 2026
**Prepared For:** Potential Sponsors & Partners

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**PikiStreams** is a next-generation, decentralized movie streaming platform designed to offer a premium, ad-free viewing experience without the subscription fatigue of modern streaming services. By leveraging Peer-to-Peer (P2P) technology and advanced content discovery algorithms, PikiStreams provides instant access to a vast library of high-definition content in a sleek, "boutique" style user interface.

We are seeking sponsorship to accelerate development, expand platform support (mobile/desktop apps), and enhance our decentralized infrastructure.

## 2. The Problem

The current streaming landscape is fragmented and frustrating:
*   **Subscription Fatigue:** Users are forced to subscribe to multiple services (Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, etc.) to access the content they want.
*   **Ad Saturation:** Free alternatives are plagued by invasive pop-ups, malware risks, and poor user interfaces.
*   **Fragmented Libraries:** No single platform hosts everything, leading to endless switching between apps.

## 3. The Solution: PikiStreams

PikiStreams solves these problems by combining the depth of the decentralized web with the polish of a premium streaming service.

### Key Features

*   **🎬 Instant P2P Streaming:** Powered by `WebTorrent`, movies start playing immediately without waiting for downloads to complete. Support for seeking and range requests ensures a smooth playback experience.
*   **✨ Boutique User Interface:** A modern, glassmorphism-inspired design that rivals top-tier streaming apps. Features include:
    *   Immersive Hero Sliders with trailers.
    *   Dynamic "Trending", "Top Rated", and "Upcoming" sections.
    *   Real-time search suggestions with rich metadata.
*   **🛡️ Ad-Free & Safe:** Our proprietary scraping engine utilizes **Puppeteer** with advanced stealth and ad-blocking plugins to sanitize content sources, ensuring users never see a pop-up or malicious ad.
*   **🧠 Intelligent Discovery:**
    *   **Smart Scraper:** automatically finds the best quality torrents (1080p/4k) with high seeder counts (10+) to ensure reliability.
    *   **Strict Name Matching:** Custom algorithms prevent incorrect files from being played, ensuring the movie you click is the movie you watch and the user safety is guaranteed.
*   **📱 Cross-Platform Ready:** Built with web technologies (Node.js, Express, HTML5) capable of being packaged for Desktop (Electron) and Mobile (Cordova).

## 4. Technical Architecture

PikiStreams is built on a robust, modern technology stack designed for performance and scalability:

*   **Frontend:** Vanilla JavaScript/CSS for maximum performance and zero bloat. Custom responsive design.
*   **Backend:** Node.js & Express.
*   **Streaming Engine:** WebTorrent (P2P protocol).
*   **Automation:** Puppeteer (Headless Chrome) for real-time content indexing.
*   **Privacy:** No user tracking or data selling.

## 5. Development Roadmap

With your support, we aim to deliver the following milestones:

*   **Phase 1: Mobile Optimization (Current Focus)** - Perfecting the experience on Android and iOS devices.
*   **Phase 2: "Hood" Social Features** - Implementing a "Neighbors" feature to see what peers are watching and share recommendations in a decentralized manner.
*   **Phase 3: AI Recommendations** - Personalized suggestions based on viewing history without compromising privacy.
*   **Phase 4: TV App** - Android TV and Firestick support for the living room experience.
